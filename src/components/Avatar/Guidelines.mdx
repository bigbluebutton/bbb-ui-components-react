# BBBAvatar guidelines

## When to use

Use BBBAvatar to show who a person is, for example in a list of people or next to a message.

Use something else when:

- The picture is not of a person: use an icon or an image.
- The person can click it: put it inside a button or a link, because the avatar has no action.

## Structure

- **Picture:** the person's image, or their initials when there is none or it fails to load.
- **Shape:** a circle, or a rounded square for moderators.
- **Color:** behind the initials, or around the image.
- **Talking ring:** optional, in the same color.
- **Tooltip:** the full name, on hover.

## Choose an avatar

- **Size:** use `small` in dense lists, `medium` in most places and `large` when the person is the focus.
- **Color:** leave it out when the app has no color for the person. The avatar then picks one from the name, so it stays the same. Use `isYou` for the current person.
- **Custom color:** make it dark enough, because the initials are always white.
- **Shape:** use `isModerator` only for moderators, because the shape is how people recognize them.

## Accessibility

- Show the name next to the avatar whenever there is room. Initials alone may match several people.
- Say in text that a person is a moderator or is talking. The avatar shows both only visually.
- An image is announced with the name. Known gap: initials are announced as letters only.
- Known gap: the tooltip appears on hover only, so it cannot be reached by keyboard.
- Known gap: the talking ring does not follow the person's reduced motion setting.
