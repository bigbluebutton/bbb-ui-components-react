# BBBTextAreaInput guidelines

## When to use

Use BBBTextAreaInput for an answer that may need several lines in a form.

Use something else when:

- The answer fits on one line: use BBBTextInput.
- The text is sent from the field itself, such as a chat message: use BBBInput.

## Structure

A single part: the field, with an optional placeholder. It has no label or helper text of its own.

## Choose the settings

- **Rows:** show about as many as the expected answer needs.
- **Length:** it stops at 250 characters unless you change `maxLength`. Say so near the field.
- **Focus:** it takes the focus as soon as it appears. Turn off `autoFocus` unless it is the only thing to do.

## Accessibility

- Add a visible label in the app and connect it with `id`, or pass `aria-label`.
- Known gap: the limit is not shown or announced.
- Known gap: the field does not grow, and text past its visible rows is hidden with no scrollbar.
