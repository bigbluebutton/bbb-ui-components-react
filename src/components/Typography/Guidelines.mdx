# BBBTypography guidelines

## When to use

Use BBBTypography for text you place yourself on a screen, such as titles, descriptions and labels, instead of setting a size and weight.

Use something else when:

- The text is part of another component, such as the label of BBButton or the title of BBBModal: that component styles its own text.
- You style the text of another element in styled-components: use `textStyles` and a color from `colors.text` (see the [Type](?path=/docs/type--docs) page).

## Choose a variant

Choose it by what the text is for, not by its size. Each variant applies one text style and a text color, the default one unless it says secondary.

- **Body text**
  - `default`: text, list and navigation items.
  - `selected`: active items and card titles.
  - `text2`: labels, descriptions, options and chat messages. Secondary color.
  - `text3`: times, numeric field labels and marks. Secondary color.
  - `large`: large menus and captions over video.
- **Headings**
  - `header`: panel and modal headers, in uppercase.
  - `section`: groups inside panels and modals.
  - `highlight`: empty states and highlight titles, one per screen.
  - `display`: timers and durations, numbers only.
- **Labels**
  - `button`: buttons and tabs.
  - `smallLabel`: names on video tiles, small badges and counters.

## Choose the element

`variant` sets how the text looks, and `as` sets what it is. It renders a `p` unless you pass `as`.

- Pass a heading element (`h1` to `h6`) for a title, so that screen readers can list it. Pick the level by the page's outline, not by the variant.
- Pass `span` for text inside a line, such as a counter next to a name.
- For the label of a form field, use the label of the field component, such as BBBTextInput.

## Do

- Use `header` once at the top of a panel or modal, and `section` for the groups below it.
- Write `header` text in normal case. The style turns it to uppercase, and screen readers read it as written.
- Use `display` only for numbers, such as `05:00`.

## Don't

- Don't use `selected` or `header` to make words stand out inside running text. Bold is for titles and selected items.
- Don't use `button` for text that cannot be clicked.
- Don't change the color with `style` or `className` to make text secondary. Use `text2` or `text3`.
- Don't pick a variant only because of its size. If no variant fits what the text is for, ask for a new one.

## Accessibility

- The secondary color of `text2` and `text3` is at least 4.5:1 on the white, light and blue backgrounds. Don't put them on a darker background or over video.
- The font comes from the app. Load Nunito Sans with the 400, 600 and 700 weights.
